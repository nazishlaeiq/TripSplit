TripSplit for Android (installable app, works offline)

1. On a computer or your phone browser, open https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag this whole folder (TripSplitPWA) onto the page. You get a link like https://something.netlify.app
3. On your Android phone open that link in Chrome.
4. Tap the 3-dot menu > "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen"). TripSplit now appears in your app list.
5. Share the same link with the other families; each installs it the same way.

Notes: data stays on each phone. Use Share tab > "Copy trip code" / "Import code" to combine expenses.
The AI tab needs internet and your own Claude API key (console.anthropic.com).
