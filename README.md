# TripSplit
TripSplit helps groups share the costs of a trip fairly. Add each expense and who paid for it, and TripSplit works out who owes whom, then suggests the fewest payments needed to settle up.

Scope of use

Who it’s for

Friends or families travelling together
Anyone splitting shared costs such as hotels, travel, food and activities

What it does

Records trip expenses with amount, date, category and who paid
Splits each expense equally between everyone, or between selected people
Shows totals, spending by category, and each person’s balance
Suggests settle-up payments
Lets you combine expenses across devices with a trip code
Offers an optional AI assistant for adding expenses in plain words

What it doesn’t do

It doesn’t move money or connect to bank accounts. Payments happen outside the app.
It doesn’t store data on a server. All trip data stays on the device where it’s entered.
It isn’t a financial, tax or accounting tool.
